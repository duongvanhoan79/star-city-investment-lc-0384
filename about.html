AboutSTAR CITY INVESTMENT LC

STAR CITY INVESTMENT LC is a U.S. 501(c)(3) public charity (EIN 16010384) formed to bring practical support to people in transition. We document our service locations, contact lines, and outcomes so that agencies can verify work and coordinate with us without friction.
